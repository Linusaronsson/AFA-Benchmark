# Synthetic MNIST (`synthetic_mnist`)

A synthetic image dataset of MNIST's shape, generated by AFABench. An
instance is one 28 × 28 image of noise with a geometric pattern of its
class drawn in the right half, and its label the class, 0–9. The left
half is only noise, so a method gains nothing by acquiring it.

Dataset generation draws the data anew for every dataset realization,
seeded by the realization index; there is no source file.

## Instances and label

10,000 instances per dataset realization (`n_samples`), each label drawn
uniformly from the 10 classes.

## Features

One image, 1 × 28 × 28, of pixels in 0–1. Every pixel starts as normal
noise with mean 0 and standard deviation 0.1 (`noise_std`). The class's
pattern is then drawn in columns 14–27 with intensity 0.8
(`pattern_intensity`), and every pixel is clipped to 0–1, so about half
the noise pixels are 0. Rows and columns count from 0 at the top left:

| Class | Pattern | Pixels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Ring | distance 4–6 from row 14, column 21 |
| 1 | Vertical bar | columns 19–22, every row |
| 2 | Horizontal bar | rows 12–15, columns 14–27 |
| 3 | Diagonal | row *j*, column 14 + *j*, for *j* = 0–13 |
| 4 | Filled rectangle | rows 8–19, columns 16–25 |
| 5 | Cross | the bars of classes 1 and 2 |
| 6 | Triangle | rows 6–21, widening downwards around column 21 |
| 7 | L | rows 6–21 of columns 16–19, and rows 18–21 of columns 16–25 |
| 8 | X | the diagonal of class 3 and its mirror image, column 27 − *j* |
| 9 | Dots | 12 dots of 2 × 2 pixels at rows 6, 10, 14, 18 and columns 16, 20, 24 |

The patterns of classes 4, 5 and 7 replace the noise under them with 0.8;
the others add 0.8 to it. Pixels of exactly 0.8 therefore identify classes
4, 5 and 7 (#152).

A selection reveals a 4 × 4 pixel patch: 49 feature groups on a 7 × 7
grid (`28x28_to_7x7` unmasker).

## Preprocessing in AFABench

- No normalization beyond the clipping.
- All feature costs are 1; a selection costs the mean of its patch's
  feature costs, so 1.
- Splits are the pipeline's: 60% train, 20% validation, 20% test, for each
  dataset realization.
- The hard budgets are 5, 10 and 15 (`all` profile, the default).
