# physionet.csv

`physionet.csv` is derived from "Predicting Mortality of ICU Patients: The
PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2012", version 1.0.0
(<https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2012/1.0.0/>): one row per ICU
stay of sets A, B and C, the mean of each variable over the stay's record,
and the stay's in-hospital death.

The data are licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0
(ODC-By, <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/>). Cite I. Silva,
G. Moody, D. J. Scott, L. A. Celi and R. G. Mark, Predicting in-hospital
mortality of ICU patients: the PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge
2012, Computing in Cardiology 39, 245–248 (2012), and PhysioNet as listed in
[`docs/reference/datasets/physionet_challenge_2012.md`](../../docs/reference/datasets/physionet_challenge_2012.md).
