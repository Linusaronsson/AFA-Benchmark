# METABRIC_RNA_Mutation.csv

`METABRIC_RNA_Mutation.csv` is version 1 of the Kaggle dataset "Breast
Cancer Gene Expression Profiles (METABRIC)" by Raghad Alharbi
(<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/raghadalharbi/breast-cancer-gene-expression-profiles-metabric>),
unchanged, made from cBioPortal's study `brca_metabric`.
