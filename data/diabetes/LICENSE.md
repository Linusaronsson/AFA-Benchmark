# diabetes.csv

`diabetes.csv` is `datasets/diabetes.csv` of
<https://github.com/iancovert/dynamic-selection>, unchanged. It holds
Kachuee et al.'s preprocessing (<https://github.com/mkachuee/Opportunistic>)
of the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 1999–2016,
public-use data of the National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS), CDC.

NHANES public-use data are generally in the public domain. Using them binds
you to the NCHS data user agreement
(<https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html>): use them only
for statistical reporting and analysis, make no attempt to identify anyone
in them, and do not link them with individually identifiable data.

Cite the papers and NHANES as listed in
[`docs/reference/datasets/diabetes.md`](../../docs/reference/datasets/diabetes.md).
