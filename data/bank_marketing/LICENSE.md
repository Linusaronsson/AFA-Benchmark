# bank-marketing.csv

`bank-marketing.csv` is the UCI Machine Learning Repository's dataset 222,
"Bank Marketing" (<https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing>),
in its `bank-full` version as `ucimlrepo` returns it, semicolon-separated.

The dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). Cite S. Moro, P. Rita and
P. Cortez, Bank Marketing [Dataset], UCI Machine Learning Repository (2014),
doi:10.24432/C5K306, and the paper listed in
[`docs/reference/datasets/bank_marketing.md`](../../docs/reference/datasets/bank_marketing.md).
